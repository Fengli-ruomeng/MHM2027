# MoreHarderMinecraft: Edge

Edge gives every player a **gateway**: a small computer in your body that you program in **Lua**. Your code can make you faster, heal you, warn you, and break into other gateways. Some mobs carry gateways too, and they will try to break into yours.

Nothing here is hidden or random. Your weaknesses come from the choices you make, and every rule is written down in this wiki.

## Quick facts

- Press **G** to open the control panel. It's where you run, stop and write programs.
- Your scripts are saved on the server, in your own storage. Only you can edit them.
- Buffer, your cyber health bar, **never refills by itself**. Only Cyber Potions refill it.
- Your gateway level is kept when you die.
- Don't want any of this? Flash **Null Firmware** and the cyber layer leaves you alone for good (see [Progression](Progression)).
